Data sources and verification
  • Site attributes (site area, GPR, estimated units, hotel rooms, commercial space, launch date, sales agent) are taken from the official 2H2026 GLS Programme release (Appendix 1, pr26-41a.pdf).
  • Locations are taken from the official site-name hyperlinks embedded in that PDF. Each link opens URA's GLS map service (eservice.ura.gov.sg/maps) for a specific site ID. The corresponding parcel polygon and attributes were read from URA's authoritative map feature service (maps.ura.gov.sg/.../lsag/ura_sale_sites) and reprojected from SVY21 (EPSG:3414) to WGS84.
  • Every parcel's official attributes (site area, GPR, units, sales agent, development type) were cross-checked against the PDF and matched exactly. All nine sites are shown as verified polygons; none are approximated.
  • Any site that could not be fully verified would be visibly flagged on its marker, card, and detail panel. No sites are currently flagged.
  • This is an informational visualisation. Official tender and planning decisions should be based on URA / MND documentation and the detailed conditions of sale for each parcel.
